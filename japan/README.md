## 1. How we like to travel (the planning rules)

Confirmed in Q&A, Oct 2026. Use these when drafting any itinerary.

1. **Slow > packed.** 5–6 stops a day is too much. A first visit to a place can be
   a bit denser, but the ideal day is *an area that's lovely to walk around*, with
   good food that we stumble on nearby.
2. **Never cross the city for one restaurant.** Japan's everyday food is great;
   eat where we are. Destination-dining = wasted time + inflated expectations.
3. **Live like a local, if only briefly.** Favourite memory: the Kitano Tenmangu
   plum festival, because we saw what locals were doing and did it with them.
   Seasonal local festivals and everyday rituals beat headline sights.
4. **Japanese culture, hands-on.** The tea ceremony was a highlight because it
   showed the culture from the inside. Open to more: zazen/meditation, temple
   experiences, crafts.
5. **Walking places win.** Nara (mostly walking around) and Kyoto were favourites.
   Tokyo is the least favourite: shopping and expensive, not memorable.
6. **Just the two of us = best days.** Otaru (6 Nov 2025) and Jozankei (7 Nov) were
   loved because we were on our own, taking our time.
7. **Onsen: yes, often, but not every night.** Prefer an onsen hotel with good
   facilities (like Onyado Nono Kyoto) over luxury. A ryokan isn't a goal in itself.
8. **Low-crowd seasons first.** Light drizzle is fine, even memorable. Avoid the
   heavy rainy season (tsuyu, ~June to mid-July).
9. **Experience first, photos second.** Own camera, shoot along the way. Don't
   plan days around photo spots.
10. **Theme parks only with a group.** No extreme rides for YT. USJ was J's thing.
11. **Manga/anime: no special trips.** Fine only if a spot happens to be on the way.
12. **Shopping: Uniqlo, GU, Onitsuka Tiger.** Keep a slot for these every trip.
    The kitchenware streets (Kappabashi in Tokyo, Doguyasuji in Osaka) are still unvisited.
13. **Transport:** trains and buses inside Tokyo. Happy to drive outside cities,
    including in snow.
14. **Budget:** ~RM 7–8k per person per trip.

Club Med cost on the voucher: $1,317.38 + $60 membership per person (currency to confirm).

### Shape of the trip
- **24–26 Mar · Tokyo, 2 nights, 1 full day (Thu 25).** Stay near Haneda
  (Hamamatsucho or Shinagawa) for the 09:30 flight on the 26th. The 25th is a good
  day for shopping (Uniqlo/GU/Onitsuka) plus Kappabashi, plus a slow, local-feeling
  area (Yanaka). Sakura in Tokyo usually *starts* around now.
- **26–30 Mar · Kiroro.** Late-March spring skiing; Kiroro keeps deep snow into April.
- **30 Mar – 4 Apr · just the two of us, 4 full days + flight day, self-drive.**
  Idea: land at Haneda 19:05 and stay nearby. Pick the car up at Haneda on the 31st,
  then return it there before the 23:30 flight on 4 Apr. No city driving, no luggage hauling.

### Sakura timing (typical years; real forecasts come out ~Jan–Feb 2027)
| Place | Usual full bloom | Fit with our dates |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo | late Mar – early Apr | ✅ 25 Mar (opening) and 30–31 Mar (near peak) |
| Fuji's south-west foothills, Shizuoka side (Fujinomiya, Fuji city) | late Mar – early Apr | ✅ good |
| Kofu basin, Yamanashi (peach blossoms in Fuefuki, Jindai-zakura in Hokuto) | early – mid Apr | 🟡 early bloom likely |
| Kawaguchiko / Chureito Pagoda (~850 m) | ~5 – 15 Apr | 🟡 may be *just* before peak; Fuji with snow still guaranteed |
| Matsumoto / Kiso Valley (Nagano) | mid Apr | ❌ probably too early |

### Draft options for 31 Mar – 4 Apr (to choose)
- **A · Fuji all-round (recommended):** 2 nights Kawaguchiko (onsen hotel with a Fuji
  view) → 2 nights on the Shizuoka side or in the Kofu basin, following wherever the
  sakura is out. Shimoyoshida and Chureito at dawn, Oshino Hakkai, Saiko Iyashi-no-Sato
  Nenba (thatched village), hoto noodles. Fujisan Hongu Sengen Taisha (the main Fuji
  shrine) + Shiraito Falls. Peach-blossom villages and wineries around Kofu.
- **B · Fuji + Izu:** 2 nights Kawaguchiko → 2 nights Izu peninsula (onsen towns
  like Shuzenji, which feels like a little Kyoto with its bamboo path; coast drive).
  Earlier spring, more onsen, sea food.
- **C · Fuji + Kamakura:** 3 nights Kawaguchiko → 1 night Kamakura/Enoshima for
  temples + coast (from the wishlist), then Haneda is ~1 h away.
