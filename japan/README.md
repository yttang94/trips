## 2. What the past plans say about us (hypotheses — confirm in Q&A)
- **Early starters.** 06:00–07:30 starts to beat crowds at Arashiyama, Fushimi Inari, Senso-ji, Hakone Shrine.
- **Culture + craft over sights-for-sights'-sake.** Booked *two* tea ceremonies; wishlist has an old print bookshop, a washi paper shop (Kyukyodo), wagashi, Kappabashi and Doguyasuji kitchen streets.
- **Quiet alternatives.** Adashino / Otagi Nenbutsu-ji picked explicitly as "away from crowds."
- **Seasonal events.** Plum festival, chrysanthemum exhibition, autumn leaves, Tori-no-Ichi on the list.
- **Food is a pillar.** Matcha (Uji), mochi, gyukatsu, wagyu, uni/seafood don, sushi, ramen, bakeries, cheesecake, parfaits.
- **Onsen stays mixed in** (Nono Kyoto, Hakone ryokan, Jozankei, Furano).
- **Photography matters** — golden-hour planning, photo spots, a hired photographer.
- **Manga/anime: light touch.** Suga Shrine, Akihabara, Slam Dunk crossing, Spirited Away spot were all "low / extra time."
- **Comfortable with a car** outside cities, trains/bikes otherwise.
- Shopping: Uniqlo/GU, Onitsuka Tiger, souvenirs — moderate.
